BoilerSimEnv: run the boiler through a 12-hour shift

The physics is the heat-loss model built from the boiler design workbook. The fuel gets wetter and drier during the shift. You, or a control policy, set the excess air every 30 minutes. The score is the gap to the best possible setting.

Assumption, not measurement: the workbook has no penalty for too little air, so a stylised rule adds one: below a minimum air (35 % plus 0.15 % per kg of extra fuel water per tonne), the efficiency drops 0.25 points for every point of air missing. Under that rule the best setting at each step is exactly that minimum. Replace the rule with a plant curve before drawing conclusions.
